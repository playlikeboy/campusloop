# CampusLoop — whole-product redesign (landing + app)

Scope: landing `/` (Persuade) and every app surface (Operate) share one world. Audience: Indian undergrads, 18–22, on mid-range Android phones, in hostel rooms, canteens and between lectures; they live on Instagram, Snapchat, WhatsApp. User asked: social & playful feel, keep logo only, simple plain copy, remove clutter and AI-slop text.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Your campus, spoken in the sticker language students already use on Stories. Polls, questions, mentions and countdowns are the real controls, not decoration. Refuses the SaaS hero + three-feature-card grid and the dark neon "gen-z" template.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #15131D text, Loop Violet (from the logo C) as the only action colour and as full-bleed story fields on landing. Colour laws: Sticker Yellow only for Loop Points, Crush Pink only for Campus Match, Mint only for verified/safe. Components are stickers: white rounded tiles with lift shadow, heavy condensed display captions (Bricolage Grotesque), poll bars that fill, question boxes with a violet header strip, @mention pills, countdown tiles. Body in Figtree. No gradients, glass or emoji-as-icons.

STORY: Visitor sees real campus moments as a story, understands "only verified students from your college are here", taps one button to join with college email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Phone-height story frame on a violet field, tap-through progress bars on top, cycling 4 example campus moments (labelled examples), each carrying a live sticker (poll, question, countdown, mention). Left/top: headline "Your campus. Only your campus." + college email CTA + college search. Mobile: story frame full width under headline.

FORM: Story-sticker UI, position 1 on the ordered list (impeccable's pick). seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
