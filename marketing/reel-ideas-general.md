# General brand reel ideas (not tied to one feature)

Each idea names the hook from `psychological-hooks.md` it's built on, a 15–30s beat sheet, and a
suggested on-screen caption. None of these name a specific feature as the hero — the app itself
(as a place, not a tool) is the hero.

## 1. "POV: it's 2 AM and your hostel wing is still awake"
- **Hook:** belonging / FOMO
- **Beats:** phone screen glow, scrolling a feed of confessions/polls from real-looking students,
  a laugh-reaction, a "same" comment. Cut to a wide shot of a dark hostel corridor — one light on.
- **Caption:** "Your college already has a group chat. You're just not in it yet."

## 2. "Four years from now, you'll search your own name"
- **Hook:** future-self flirting
- **Beats:** a montage that *looks* like old photos but is actually in-app posts/confessions/
  event check-ins, aging the UI slightly frame to frame (sepia creep). End on a plain text card.
- **Caption:** "Start the thing you'll want to scroll back through."

## 3. "Rating my college's CampusLoop vs. actually walking to the notice board"
- **Hook:** curiosity gap / reciprocity
- **Beats:** split screen — one side sprinting across campus to a bulletin board, other side
  calmly scrolling. Comedic timing, loser is obvious before they "lose."
- **Caption:** "We already did the walking."

## 4. "Things nobody tells you before first semester" (talking-head, campus senior)
- **Hook:** identity rehearsal / reciprocity
- **Beats:** rapid-fire listicle delivered to camera, each tip ends with "...and yeah, it's on
  CampusLoop" as a throwaway line, never the subject of a sentence.
- **Caption:** "Wish someone told me this in week one."

## 5. "Guess which one of these is fake" (confession screenshots)
- **Hook:** curiosity gap / social proof
- **Beats:** 4 blurred confession-style posts on screen, countdown timer, reveal which one is
  staged vs. a real anonymized post from the platform.
- **Caption:** "Three of these are real. Can you tell?"

## 6. "The group project got easier when everyone was already in one place"
- **Hook:** belonging, low friction
- **Beats:** chaotic multi-app screen recording (5 different apps, missed messages) match-cut to
  one calm screen.
- **Caption:** "Not an ad. Just tired of six different group chats."

## 7. "Main character energy starter pack: college edition"
- **Hook:** identity rehearsal
- **Beats:** aesthetic starter-pack format (a mug, a lanyard, a laptop sticker, a phone screen —
  the phone screen is the only in-app shot, shown last and briefly).
- **Caption:** "The starter pack writes itself."

## 8. "Things that are free that nobody told you about" (anti-feature-dump format)
- **Hook:** reciprocity, FOMO
- **Beats:** fast list format, each item a surprising free benefit of campus life stacked with
  one CampusLoop beat buried in the middle, never first or last.
- **Caption:** "Free > free with an asterisk."

## Shot-list discipline for whoever films these
- Never open on the app icon or splash screen — open on a face, a hallway, a feeling.
- The product UI should read like a glimpse, not a demo — 2–3 seconds max per screen recording.
- Every caption should work as a stand-alone tweet with zero context about what CampusLoop is.
