# Feature-anchored reel ideas

Unlike the general reels, these are allowed to show a feature doing its job — but the hook still
has to land in the first 2 seconds before the feature appears. "Here's a feature" is never the
opening line.

## Academic Vault / modules + course-player upload (the feature just shipped)
1. **"I turned my messy WhatsApp notes into an actual course"**
   - Hook: identity rehearsal / reciprocity.
   - Beats: chaos montage of a WhatsApp chat full of PDFs and forwarded notes → cut to uploading
     those same files into modules (Module 1, Module 2...) → cut to the Udemy-style player with
     the lecture video + notes underneath. End on the share screen: "Share with my batch."
   - Caption: "Stopped forwarding PDFs. Started building a course."

2. **"POV: you find out the senior's 'secret notes folder' is actually public now"**
   - Hook: FOMO / curiosity gap.
   - Beats: dramatic reveal of a public resource page with modules, a YouTube lecture playing
     inline, verified checkmark on the uploader. No voiceover — just reaction-face + on-screen text.
   - Caption: "The notes folder isn't a secret anymore."

3. **"Rate my exam prep setup" (study-with-me format)**
   - Hook: social proof / identity rehearsal.
   - Beats: typical study-with-me aesthetic (coffee, lo-fi, desk shot) but the "notes app" on
     screen is the course player with the notes panel under a lecture video.
   - Caption: "The notes panel under the video is doing the heavy lifting."

4. **"I uploaded one page of notes and it already has views"**
   - Hook: social proof / reciprocity.
   - Beats: fast upload flow (title → drop a file → publish), cut straight to the "🎉 You just
     helped future students" share screen and a view counter ticking up.
   - Caption: "Didn't expect people to actually need Unit 3."

## Verified campus match / confessions / communities
5. **"Things I only post anonymously"**
   - Hook: belonging, curiosity gap.
   - Beats: text-to-speech reading of a relatable, safe-for-brand confession, UI shown only as a
     blurred background.
   - Caption: "Said with my whole chest. Anonymously."

6. **"We let an AI guess which college you're from based on your confessions"**
   - Hook: curiosity gap, identity.
   - Beats: gamified reveal format, screen-record of scrolling a campus-specific feed.
   - Caption: "It got it in four posts."

## Free courses (CS50, NPTEL, etc.) — used as proof, not the headline
7. **"Why pay for a certificate when the actual Harvard course is free here"**
   - Hook: reciprocity / value contrast.
   - Beats: price comparison graphic (bootcamp price vs. ₹0), then a quick scroll through the
     course catalog, ending on a certificate screen.
   - Caption: "Harvard didn't charge for this one. Why would we."

## Production note
Keep the "including this" rule: whenever a new feature ships, write ONE reel for it within the
same week using this file's format — specific enough to show the thing working, but still opening
on a feeling or a relatable moment, never on the feature name.
