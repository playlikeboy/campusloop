# Persuasive landing-page copy directions

Rule from `psychological-hooks.md`: never lead with one feature. The headline sells a feeling or
an identity; the feature list (confessions, communities, campus match, events, courses, notes)
only shows up as supporting proof further down the page.

## Hero headline options
1. **"The college you're already part of — now in one app."** *(belonging / already-in-motion)*
2. **"Four years go fast. This is where you'll remember them."** *(future-self flirting)*
3. **"Everyone from your batch is already here. You're the one scrolling the App Store."**
   *(FOMO, slightly teasing)*
4. **"Not another app for college. The app for your college."** *(identity, possessive framing)*
5. **"1,400+ campuses. One of them is yours."** *(scale as social proof, curiosity — which one)*

## Subheads (pair with any hero above)
- "A real feed, real confessions, real people from your own college — verified, not guessed."
- "Everything you'd ask a senior for, minus the awkward DM."
- "Free to look. Free to stay. Verify your college email only when you want the checkmark."

## Section framing (order matters — proof after the pitch, not before)
1. Hero (identity/belonging hook, no feature names)
2. "What's already happening on your campus" — live-feeling social proof strip (confession
   snippets, event RSVPs, a trending note) presented as a glimpse, not a tour
3. "Bring your notes. Leave with everyone else's." — soft intro to the academic vault, framed as
   a two-way exchange, not a resource library
4. "Find your people" — campus match / communities, framed around belonging, not swiping
5. FAQ — this is where it's finally okay to be literal and list what the app does

## CTA button copy (avoid "Sign Up")
- "See your campus first" (low-commitment entry, matches Preview Mode)
- "I'm in" (identity-affirming, casual)
- "Find my college" (search-first entry, curiosity)

## Micro-copy for the "Preview mode" banner (ties to the app change in this same release)
- Before: *"Preview mode · Verify your college email to post, vote & chat."* (implies you're
  locked out — not true, and it reads as a wall instead of an invite)
- After: *"You have full access as a guest student · Verify your college email for a verified ✓
  badge & campus match."* (reciprocity — tell them what they already have before asking for
  anything, and make the ask small: a badge, not basic access)

## What NOT to do
- Don't open with "Free courses, notes and PYQs" as the primary promise — it undersells the
  product to a single academic-resources use case and reads like a file-sharing site, not a
  campus-wide social home. Keep courses/notes as proof points, never the headline (see the
  landing metadata fix applied alongside this file).
- Don't use urgency language that isn't true ("Only 100 spots left") — exclusivity should come
  from real scale/verification framing ("1,400+ colleges," "verified students only"), not fake
  scarcity.
